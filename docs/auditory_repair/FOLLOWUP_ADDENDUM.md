# Mechanism and nonlinear follow-up after the extended screen

The completed v2 screen showed a small positive V-given-A joint-selector point estimate, with a positive fixed-OOF interval, but a negative quality-adjusted point estimate. These viewed outcomes motivate this explicitly subsequent diagnostic; it is not a new confirmatory test. Both original primary tasks (A and V given observed A) are retained, not just the favorable task.

Separate the six Q fields into technical availability (accepted count, rejected fraction, recording duration and counts of both literal codes) and amplitude (median maximum scalp peak-to-peak). Within the same five repetitions of the same nested identity splits, fit the same clinical families and all five banks with each Q set. Report each Q-only baseline, its EEG increment and the already completed full-Q result. Amplitude is not assumed to be exclusively artifact, and adjusting for it may remove relevant biological variation.

Also fit one bounded nonlinear EEG alternative on both tasks: a centered Gaussian kernel with training-median squared-distance bandwidth, added to the clinical linear/quadratic kernel. Standardization, bandwidth and centering are training-only. Use the existing clinical penalties1/10/100 and EEG penalties0.01/0.1/1/10, including the exact clinical-only option. Bank and penalties are selected in the inner folds. This tests a limited nonlinear association; it is not a new raw-EEG encoder or independent validation. Complete both tasks regardless of outcome. Preserve all model-selection failures and contrasts.

For the separate mixed-MFF archival fit, use the earliest uniquely linked candidate record and target-specific nonconflicting raw-score masks. No normalization of raw0–40 values is performed. A/V predictions use a conservative0–100 nonnegative range rather than claiming a resolved0–40 scale maximum; CAP/SIR use0–9/1–5. Use5 repeated5-fold/3-inner-fold candidate-isolated validation, train-only missing-value processing, measured-age clinical fields only when actually available, source/paradigm categories, technical-quality controls, a fixed three-value stad amplitude bank, and a shuffled-training EEG control. This cannot establish an increment beyond unmeasured clinical variables and is not a CI-only analysis. Explicit CI support is separately stated.

No significance/effect-size threshold stops any of these declared analyses. Additional fits are limited to80,000 for HA mechanism/nonlinear diagnostics and40,000 for mixed-MFF, subject to the whole-round300,000-attempt operational budget. These additions are interpretable sensitivity experiments, not automatic architecture/seed searches for a favorable result.
