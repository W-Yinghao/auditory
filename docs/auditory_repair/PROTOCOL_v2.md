# PTA repair and extended archival exploration v2

The user requests thorough continuation, with conservative decisions to stop: absence of a positive interval is not grounds to discard an informative question. This version completes the entire declared analysis set regardless of effect direction. It records continuous estimates, uncertainty, fold stability, measurement reliability and alternative explanations. It does not lower the standard for claiming clinical validity or invent missing visit/device/scale semantics.

1. Correct the original PTA source-row mapping and quantify differences in legacy clinical support and support-balanced folds. Preserve historical files. Replay historical D readouts within their original clinical cohort/folds using correctly linked PTA, training-only missing-value handling, and the existing correctly scoped EEG projections. New clinical support and changed folds cannot silently reuse an encoder trained on their validation subjects.
2. Reanalyse the historical Phase 3 question using its original cohort and folds, keeping corrected PTA missing rather than borrowing another child's value. Reproduce old predictions before interpreting corrected ones. Report historical shared-penalty models and the separately penalized sensitivity.
3. Build an expanded, correctly linked earliest-record HA cohort from all metadata-qualified original rows. At least two accepted trials of each literal code are needed to compute the declared two-condition summaries; fewer observations remain a support description rather than an assertion of no value. No per-code40/total64/participant30 scientific cutoff applies here.
4. Compare five fixed record-level banks: 60 Hjorth summaries; two literal conditions' 20-channel poststimulus 50 ms means over 0–400 ms (320 dimensions); code2−code1 contrast (160); prestimulus condition means over −200–0 ms (160); and median poststimulus within-epoch spatial correlations plus log channel variances (210). Use at most256 chronologically uniform accepted epochs. No peak/window/channel/condition-semantic search occurs. Contrast signs use literal codes and are not called MMN. Short epochs are not treated as resting-state spectra.
5. Describe interleaved and temporal-half reliability of every bank at32/64/128/256 trials using cross-identity feature correlations and recorded support. Spearman–Brown values are only split-half heuristics; temporal stationarity and adjacency are not assumed. Reliability never selects a favorable feature subset.
6. Test A, V and V given observed A, plus prespecified supplementary CAP and SIR source-column endpoints. A and V given A remain the primary questions. CAP/SIR regressions measure prediction error in their original ordered score units; no new diagnostic cutoffs or mechanistic profile separation is asserted. Include ceiling fractions, clinical target relationships, and age/duration/ceiling-stratified OOF error as descriptive diagnostics, not favorable-subgroup model searches.
7. Use five repeated5-fold outer and3-fold inner identity-isolated splits. Clinical linear/quadratic families and both penalties are chosen only within training. Include mean, clinical-only, each clinical+bank model, EEG-only bank selection, joint inner bank selection, quality-adjusted joint selection, and train-only shuffled-EEG joint selection. Combined models include the clinical-only option. Every fold is retained. The best bank on outer results is never rebranded as the primary model: the joint selector is itself evaluated out of fold.
8. Report paired MAE gains, 2,000 identity bootstrap intervals, all five repeat gains, leave-one-identity sensitivity, non-ceiling/ceiling and fixed age/device-duration strata. Identity errors are averaged across repeats before bootstrap; repeats do not multiply the sample size. These intervals do not incorporate resampling and refitting the entire workflow, and multiple exploratory comparisons require cautious interpretation.
9. Examine CI/MFF records separately, including available within-record discrimination/reliability, genuine task pairs and repeated archived measurements. Small groups may yield descriptive findings; undefined clinical timing or unidentified score units cannot be repaired with a statistical threshold. Keep explicit CI, CIHA, clinical-history clues and unknown sources distinct. Any broad mixed-MFF association is a separate source-stratified archival estimand, never a CI-only result.

The first-pass v1 outcomes and its stricter advancement rule remain historical. V2 replaces that rule for new work with complete planned exploration, including small positive or uncertain effects. No automatic architecture search follows an observed winner, and no clinical claim follows merely from running more models. All computation and tests use Slurm; the current set needs no GPU. Operational limits in the JSON config protect against accidental runaway jobs, not against unpromising scientific estimates. Private source rows, dates, identities, predictions and parameters remain private. Nothing is pushed automatically.
