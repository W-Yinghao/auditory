Result-informed design, before this lane's outputs. CBraMod pretrained3e-4 has a positive matched mean advantage over L1, while random and REVE contrasts are smaller/uncertain. This motivates strong direct comparators, not a favorable test-side model selection.

Thirty bundles: 5 outer person folds ×2 content folds ×3 seeds. Reuse the completed outer L1, CB pretrained/random3e-4 and REVE pretrained1e-5 sources. Their 120 independently trained inner-person sources come from the frozen data_inner_v1 plan. No new encoder fit is duplicated here. Inner people=(outer fold+1)%5; they are excluded from encoder updates, early stopping, target coordinates, ridge/bridge fitting and temperature calibration. Development uses only original source-ES sounds. Original outer-test sounds are not used in selection. Outer models are the existing outer-train refits. Generic pretraining identity overlap remains uncertified.

Each of the three local/FM pairs retains both original direct native and unified outputs, and the inner-selected strong single among all four models is reported without test-person-specific choice. Candidate families:

- Convex probabilities with local weight0,.25,.5,.75,1, separately for the two readouts; candidate sound pools are identical.
- SC-remapped local and common-sound linear fusion of concatenated local/FM representations. Ridge alpha=.1,10,1000, fit only source-training representations; output is the same fixed32-dimensional sound-head coordinate as native NCE. Source-head outputs must agree numerically across branches, otherwise execution fails.
- A zero-output linear residual from FM32 to the local fixed-sound32 coordinate. Initial matrix/bias zero; normalize(local+residual). Adam1e-3, batch512 sampled anchors,100 steps with candidates0,20,50,100. No backbone update. This is actual residual optimization with an exact epoch-zero candidate; no latent addition between unrelated CS coordinates.

All heads use the same deterministic400 source-fit/ES segments as the original readout. For each representation candidate the unified ridge is fitted/calibrated on those source-training people with the original alpha/temperature grids. Source-ES reuse in ridge/temperature fitting is disclosed; the independent inner people remain untouched by all fitting. Strong-single, alpha, residual step and probability weight selection use equal-weight inner-person true native NLL (unified weight selection uses unified NLL), with deterministic simpler-candidate tie breaking. All chosen candidates are evaluated on every eligible outer person's full test query set. Never choose on outer loss, hearing-group effects or significance.

Diagnostics compare each residual state to the direct local representation: relative raw residual, parallel/orthogonal components, pre/post-normalization displacement, angle, native/unified score changes, probability total variation and argmax changes. A forward-time mismatched FM sensitivity holds local fixed. It is a test intervention, not proof of causal information source.
