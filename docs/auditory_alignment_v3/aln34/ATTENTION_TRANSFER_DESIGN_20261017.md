The matched DTU CBraMod/local retrieval results and corrected legacy attention-order results are already known. None of the attention-transfer values below has been inspected. Retrieval improvement does not establish attention improvement. This new version reuses frozen single-speaker retrieval encoders and explicitly tests transfer to competing-speaker attention; it does not claim attention-supervised encoder training.

Use the 120 DTU and 40 FAU source/inner pairs already registered in the new adaptation-policy plans: local, CB pretrained/random, representative REVE pretrained; DTU seeds3401/3407/3413, FAU3401, all registered subject/content folds. Inner encoders exclude both the outer and inner people; outer encoders exclude outer people. The inherited source recipe was selected in earlier research, so the nested claim covers the currently evaluated pipeline, not all historical research choices.

For each inner and outer encoder, reuse its training-side native/unified readout, learned only on source single-speaker fit/es data. Compute attended-minus-ignored scores on competing-speaker segments, preserving participant, trial, physical start, content role and candidate order. Candidate audio is used to score EEG outputs, never as decoder input.

Two integrators are retained for every duration/readout. Both report paired-order2AFC accuracy, ties0.5, and probability NLL in bits. An unusable learned fit is explicitly unavailable, never replaced by a successful-looking value; mean remains valid even with zero score scale (zero logits, chance/tie).

Select mean or learned integrator separately for each duration/readout using equal-person inner-development attention NLL, on inner people and source-ES competing content. Exact ties favor mean. Report this entire-policy choice against fixed local and fixed CBpre on identical outer windows; do not select with outer attention, retrieval test scores or group differences.

Native/unified, accuracy/NLL and5/10/20s are separate labelled endpoints.
