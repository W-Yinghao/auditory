# New-lane aggregation note — 2026-10-17

Written after registration and engineering probes, before inspecting new production effect estimates. Known results are the completed historical TF/local, D, E, SCI and complement panels; the new data/inner status has five complete units, but their effect estimates have not been read. This is a result-informed exploratory extension, not a retroactive preregistration. Its estimands do not change any training plan or selection rule.

All planned units, probes, failures and missing units remain visible. Production summaries reject truncated/probe records and require matching plan rows and provenance. Never promote a probe to a scientific observation. An inner-source checkpoint is a dependency, not an outer-test result.

An independent identity is the sampling unit. For each declared contrast, require every planned content cell and record/session for that identity at the reported seed(s), on both sides. Average records within content cell, content cells within seed, then seeds within identity. Report each seed separately and the complete common multi-seed panel; no available-seed averaging, zero filling or multiplication of n by seeds/folds. Federici uses the existing entity manifest, excludes the one phantom from human inference and keeps HC-v identities separate from HC. Report incomplete paired coverage, not a full-population claim.

Use the existing SCI_R1 identity bootstrap-t (4,000 replicates), with whole vectors resampled together and simultaneous max-t intervals within the explicitly named dataset × intervention/model × readout/metric × seed-scope × reported-population family. Each hearing group's descriptive family is separate from the all-person family; this first release does not infer group differences or claim correction across groups. Intervals condition on fitted models and shared training data; they are not uncertainty over a fresh training population. Groups are reported symmetrically. No choice of model, seed, time cell, group or support fraction is made using these outputs. Snapshots during completion are descriptive interim monitoring, not sequential significance tests.

Budget: preserve source-ES-selected checkpoints, all fixed .25/.5/1/2× trajectory checkpoints and the historical original schedule separately. Positive risk improvement means R(reference)−R(candidate). Main intervention comparison is the new source-selected doubled-horizon model versus the historical source-selected model; fixed 2× versus fixed 1× describes the new horizon only. Compare pretrained/random at matched architecture/lr/budget and retain L1. Data axes: compare each .25/.5 subset with its own original full-data source; separate people from content, and retain fixed-epoch/variable-step exposure caveats. Inner-source evaluations are counted but excluded from scientific outer-test summaries.

D policy: summarize all independently inner-selected adapter × support policies versus the unchanged base on identical outer queries. Keep native and unified true NLL separate from mismatch gain and rank. Fusion: preserve original direct local/FM, inner-selected strong single and each selected probability/SC/common-linear/residual candidate. Compare each candidate with all applicable direct references; summarize residual/normalization/prediction diagnostics separately. Do not choose an outer-test best fusion. Episode: compare inner-selected episode and ordinary continuations with each other and source/no-adaptation, and retain all fixed source/regime/support-step candidates. A selected source fallback is a valid result.

Content/time: retain the complete train-time × test-time matrices, direct and conditional targets, model and strict raw-window references, correct and same-person segment-mismatched predictions. Correlation is reported on its original scale and conditional on the declared target features; no peak-based neural latency or causal positive-minus-negative subtraction. Private: preserve current-class AUC/NLL/Brier, history and swap increments, all masked-time cells and fixed 0/5/20-step support candidates. Fixed private candidates are not an inner-selected deployment policy. The historical private FM initialization contrasts are recipe contrasts. Never use test AUC to flip a score.

The first aggregator release covers budget, reduced-data, new D, strong fusion and episode efficacy. Content/time and private science get distinct matrix/developmental summaries with the same identity rules; this note does not claim those implementations or experiments are finished. Age/device-duration/group analyses must be declared with concrete covariates, valid missingness and corrected families before their new results are inspected.
