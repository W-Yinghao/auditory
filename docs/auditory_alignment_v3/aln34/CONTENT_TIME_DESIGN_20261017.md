# S34_CONTENT_TIME — fixed-model decoding and shifted-access generalization

2026-10-17, result-informed extension after SCI_R2, DTU true-full paired evidence and the completed old complement functional evaluation. All fixed candidates are analysed, regardless of group/age significance. Sources: L1, true-full CB pretrained/random3e-4, representative REVEpre1e-5; all5 person folds, both content folds for DTU/FAU and the existing single Federici content fold. DTU has3 seeds; FAU/Federici seed3401. Total180 frozen-model evaluations. No encoder training is repeated. Public auditory-prior representations will be added under a separate source manifest when available, without making their performance an entry gate.

Read out SCI_R2's available sound features from EEG representations: env,onset,spec,flux,Ac,Lz. Federici has envelope only; do not invent absent features. Report direct target decoding and linear residual-target decoding after the preceding feature families in this declared order. Earlier sound attributes are used to DEFINE the target residual, with the nuisance map fitted/selected on source fit/ES only; they are never fed to the EEG decoder at training or query time. This is a descriptive conditional target, not a claim of uniquely semantic information or an independent language source. For env, add a separately labelled residual target conditional on past envelope at20,100,200,500ms; this diagnoses sound-history predictability, not a causal subtraction.

For each unit, sample400 source-training fit/ES segments deterministically with seed+44000; all eligible outer-test segments are evaluated. Eight anchors uniformly spaced0.8..3.6s in each5s segment keep every direct window inside the segment. Fit ridge on fit anchors, choose relative ridge1e-3..1e4 using source-ES correlation, refit on fit+ES. The encoder has seen these source people; this readout selection is not whole-pipeline inner-person development. Outer people and test sounds stay excluded from all fit/selection and feature residualization. Means/scales of target dimensions use source-training only.

Shifted-access temporal generalization uses EEG window centers−200,0,200,400,600ms relative to each sound anchor. Fit at each center and evaluate at all five centers. Each frozen L1/FM representation still sees [center−400,center+600)ms, so these are overlapping one-second access windows, not isolated neural latencies. The L1 unit additionally reports a20ms raw-channel mean at each center as the strict-local reference, with identical rows/targets. Do not compare labels like early/late across cohorts without their actual milliseconds/frequency support. Federici remains limited to its original2–8Hz cache.

Report per-identity mean-dimension Pearson r and standardized-target MSE for each cell, plus an EEG-only mismatch obtained by rolling whole segments within the same person while retaining the sound target. Identity-level inference averages content folds/seeds, keeps phantom outside human inference and never pairs HC with HC-v. Primary intervals are simultaneous across predeclared model/attribute/time contrasts; pointwise curves are descriptive. No test-side sign flip or best-time choice. Aggregate hearing groups and continuous age/stage only where metadata/support are valid; these are associations, not treatment effects.

The original source cache is noncausally filtered. Segment guards remove direct cross-segment windows, but cannot undo historical filtering; negative-center readability is not a pure anticipatory response. A new raw-source causal-filter sensitivity and waveform-derived F0/voicing require separate versioned inputs and are NOT claimed complete here. Existing SCI_R2 forward and band-matched raw results remain the reference; this decoding extension does not retroactively alter them.

Derived arrays/checkpoint hashes and per-identity outputs remain under the established private /projects store; summaries follow current publication scope. Smoke covers all4 architectures/initialization arms on DTU and envelope-only Federici with20 source segments,one test person,two segments and3 anchors, permanently excluded from production. Production requires the matching successful smoke receipt and unchanged source hashes, not a decoding threshold.
