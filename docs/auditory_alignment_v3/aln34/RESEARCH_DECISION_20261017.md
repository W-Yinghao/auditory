# ALN3.4 research decision and next interventions — 2026-10-17

Codex decision after the researcher explicitly requested scientific judgement, experiment design/submission and a sufficient GPU backlog. This is result-informed exploratory design. It does not change earlier registration identities or claim independent replication from seeds.

## Evidence available before this decision

- Matched CBraMod one-step true-full at 3e-4, seed 3401, lowers unified true-query risk relative to same-access L1 by 0.144 bits on FAU (67 identities), 0.213 on DTU (44), and 0.196 on Federici (86 human identities). These are the existing complete seed-3401 comparisons, not a completed three-seed result. The latest panel is still running; its interim report is being refreshed in CPU job 1043925.
- DTU random CBraMod at 1e-5 was much weaker than at 3e-4. A fixed-budget initialization advantage therefore leaves optimization speed versus transferable structure unresolved. REVE's DTU advantage over L1 is not established under the existing recipes; its larger parameter count and different epoch budget make it a useful representative comparator, not a reason for an unrestricted backbone sweep.
- D_ADAPT true-risk re-evaluation confirms both benefit and harm. Three-seed input-spatial mean delta R: FAU 10% +0.093, 30% +0.250; DTU 10% -0.208, 30% +0.150 bits/query. DTU 10% also worsens native true risk (-0.595), so bridge drift alone cannot explain it. A full 64x64 map with 4–9 support segments and a step rule selected at 30% support is a concrete competing explanation.
- Old SC results do not beat the original direct local output; RS often changes representation/predictions without a material true-risk gain. Frozen-FM context and ridge-only OOF are insufficient tests of strong standalone true-full FM fusion or whole-pipeline held-out-person selection. The 224-unit functional evaluation is running without retraining.
- Private E AUC is comparatively stable across three seeds, while REVE CS_SINGLE has large probability-calibration failures. Its rank improvement over a near-chance random comparator is not clinical usefulness or evidence of a causal mechanism. The existing 64 children are repeatedly reused; new seeds are not new children.

## Decisions

1. **Continue matched true-full CBraMod and local, with a bounded learning-budget intervention first.** Run DTU, all 5 subject folds x 2 content folds, seeds 3401/3407/3413, CBraMod pretrained/random at shared backbone LR 3e-4, representative REVE pretrained/random at shared LR 1e-5, and same-access L1. Double each method's existing epoch budget (CB 100->200; REVE 50->100; L1 200->400), retaining the existing architecture, target, optimizer, numeric LR, development criterion and outer tests. This gives 150 new units and reuses the 150 existing full-data baselines. The choice of DTU is informed by the strongest optimization/small-support ambiguity, not hearing-group significance.
2. **Measure training trajectories without pretending missing historical states exist.** Save and evaluate states at 0.25, 0.5, 1 and 2 times the old epoch budget along each new doubled-horizon trajectory. The cosine horizon changes, so its 1x state is not the old 1x recipe. Keep the old selected-model result as a separate reference. Timepoints are descriptive fixed states; outer test never chooses the checkpoint, duration or continuation. Report exact epochs, optimizer updates, exposed segments and both native/unified risks. Across architectures epoch counts are not matched updates; initialization pairs within each architecture are matched. Population/content axes remain separate forthcoming comparisons.
3. **Continue adaptation after changing the actual uncertainty source.** Next register diagonal and rank-4 identity-initialized spatial adapters, the full map and output-low-rank comparator, with support-specific development on true risk, fixed 70% queries and paired source dependencies. Add support/query episodic source training versus ordinary source training with matched exposure. Preserve old D holds; do not replay the obsolete fixed-grid policies.
4. **Continue the public auditory prior independently.** Its donor/target exclusion, matched external sound coordinates and bridge contract answer a different question from generic-FM initialization. FIXC and production core/rich manifests are complete; actual M12 and M13 remain required before its 1,154 production rows. Favorable target scores are not a launch condition.
5. **Diagnose private E calibration before broader private FM repeats.** First compare stored class-kernel and positive-affine NLL on all CS panel units; then re-evaluate saved models with leave-one-out reference scores on calibration trials. Keep both versions. A shared-LR random/pretrained private contrast and the authorized developmental/current-class/history analyses remain distinct next experiments.

## How a specific configuration will be continued or retired

Complete the declared paired experiment without a performance entry gate. Assess actual risk change and its uncertainty, stability across the same identities/seeds, scientific question answered, failure mechanism and GPU cost together. If the doubled-budget random model closes the initialization gap, focus that configuration's claim on sample/optimization efficiency and test lower-data settings; do not claim unique auditory structure. If a gap remains under paired exposure, test transfer and content dependence next; this still does not identify a neural mechanism. If a fusion recipe only remaps a stronger standalone signal without improving risk, retire that recipe's repeats and retain the direct outputs. If support-specific low-capacity adapters remove harm but add little benefit, retain the safe reference and study adaptation-ready source training; do not extrapolate to all personal adaptation.

No universal absence/ceiling conclusion follows from these decisions. Additional independent people/content remains distinct evidence. GPU scheduling stays at at most 10 running cards, with public prior up to 4; maintain queued ready work and dependencies rather than duplicate finished jobs. No scontrol.
