## 3. Federici (UPDATE_NOW; docs/02 v3.1)

Already in force since R_FEDERICI (2026-10-10, `FEDERICI_REAGGREGATION_RECEIPT.md`): HC-v are separate identities with their
own metadata rows (ages from `Nvocod` rows, recorded as inferred by order), no same-child HC–HC-v pairing, the nine Artifact
sessions are one phantom outside human inference, 86 children. The ALN2 loader (`auditory_alignment/data.py`) still groups a
same-numbered HC and HC-v recording into one fold unit and carries the HC age on the HC-v record; ages are not used by any
training, teacher-selection or sampling path (only by post-hoc analyses, which take the corrected manifest), and the fold
grouping only over-groups (no leakage), so no unit is retrained (docs/15 §2, "scope a bug to its affected dependencies").
Changing the loader would re-split Federici and give every Federici row a new execution hash; not done.
