# Original Idea → Evidence Map

This is a document-grounded feasibility matrix. It preserves goals stated in the restart brief, analysis plan, legacy corrections, and reference review; it does not add new study aims. Historical results and reference papers are leads or audit anchors, not verified current-data findings.

| Original target | Basis in the supplied material | Evidence required before the target is supportable |
|---|---|---|
| Measure auditory EEG responses reliably in children | Brief §1 and §4.5; plan §2 measurement level; legacy review F11 | Format/provenance and event alignment; readable signals; outcome-blinded QC; valid trial counts; individual waveforms; split-half or repeated-trial error; missingness and non-identifiable-feature rates. Reliability is within the stated acquisition unless cross-visit timing is established. |
| Relate EEG responses to age, device-use experience, and hearing status | Brief §1, §4.4; plan §§1, 3.3 | Confirm age and duration definitions/units, hearing measures and audibility, device state/configuration, protocol and acquisition covariates, common support, child/visit identity, and prespecified EEG feature/model. Result remains observational conditional association; no causal rehabilitation claim. |
| Study “experience and maturation” / individual differences at similar experience | Plan §1; legacy F9/F10 corrections | Age, elapsed use, age-at-fitting implication, and recording conditions must be jointly observed with informative within-range variation. Repeated dates and measurement timing are required for trajectories; a low age-duration correlation alone is insufficient. |
| Relate EEG to everyday auditory/speech function (MUSS, IT-MAIS/MAIS, SIR, CAP) | Brief cohort table; plan §§2–3.1; legacy F11 | Verify instrument version, score construction/range, assessment timing, scale ceiling, rater/source and units; obtain trustworthy EEG–clinical links and one prespecified endpoint. Compare a defensible clinical baseline with EEG using child-level identical splits; association or incremental prediction is not concurrent validity or prognosis. |
| HA primary storyline: EEG information about device experience and function | Plan §1 and §3.1; brief Cohort B opportunity | Reconcile HA records/children, earliest/index rule or confirmed dates, device-on state and audibility, complete clinical fields, sufficient EEG support, and selective missingness. Preserve >60-month records for scope checks; do not silently use historical 0–60 filtering. |
| Cohort A CI/NH stimulus processing, including within-child comparisons | Brief Cohort A opportunities; plan §4; legacy correction that scales are not the only route | Confirm child pairing across pure tones, syllables, lexical tones and device configurations; physical stimulus parameters, ear, trial counts, event codes and device artifacts. Use within-child or child-level splits. Different acoustics cannot be interpreted as language-specific processing without controls. |
| HA–NH or CI-related group comparison / migration of a common question | Brief cohort descriptions; plan §4.2 and §4.4 | Establish comparable protocol, stimulus level/audibility, age and hearing coverage, device state, reference/channel/sampling/batch, and independent child identity. Do not concatenate cohorts as external validation or treat group labels as impairment severity. |
| Distinguish stimulus responses from trial inefficiency and device artifacts | Brief §4.5 and §5; plan §§3.2 and 5; reference review CI comparator | Verify trigger/event timing, SOA and standard/deviant meaning; report valid trials and block coverage; inspect saturation, bad channels, mains/device-locked activity, boundaries, frontal/other controls and preprocessing sensitivity. A peak, decoding result, or difference wave alone cannot establish cortical origin. |
| Standard/deviant response or MMN/P1 interpretation | Brief terminology and §4.3; plan §3.2; legacy correction of fixed MMN window | Demonstrate the actual paradigm and event semantics from annotations, trigger channel, event files and logs; account for acoustic differences, adaptation and timing. Define windows from outcome-blinded QC; do not call every deviant-minus-standard waveform pure MMN or fix a child P1 at 100 ms in advance. |
| Foundation model / deep-learning extension (LiteBiMamba, EEGNet, or another network) | Brief §1 and §6; plan §3.4, §7; legacy correction of “four-tone decoding only” | Only after a clear question, sufficient usable children, trustworthy labels/conditions, simple interpretable baselines, and credible child-level validation. Fit preprocessing, representation, feature selection and tuning inside training folds; compare against fixed baselines. If these conditions do not emerge, omit the model phase. |

The reference review supplies methodological or provenance clues only. The two pediatric CI papers have overlapping EGI/odd-ball/task descriptions, but no reviewed paper proves any current file’s identity, event-code meaning, device state, or child/session mapping. Those facts still require file-level P0 evidence.
